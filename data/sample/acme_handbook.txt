# Acme Corporation — Employee Handbook (Extract)

## 1. Refund Policy

Acme's refund policy applies to all physical products sold through the
official Acme store. Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of the
date of purchase for products in their original packaging and unused
condition. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 to 7
business days of receiving the returned item.

Digital downloads and online courses are non-refundable once the license key
or access link has been delivered to the buyer, except where the product is
found to be defective.

To begin a refund, contact the Acme customer care team at support@acme.example
with your order number. Provide the order number, the purchased item, and the
reason for the return. Acme aims to acknowledge refund requests within 2
business days and will ask for evidence about the external damage, if any.

## 2. Shipping Policy

Standard shipping on orders over $75 is free across the United States. Orders
placed before 2 PM Eastern Time are dispatched the same business day. Express
shipping is available for an additional fee and guarantees delivery within 2
to 3 business days. International shipping is available to most countries and
takes between 7 and 21 business days depending on customs.

## 3. Product Warranty

Every Acme gadget ships with a 12-month limited warranty covering defects in
materials and workmanship under normal use. The warranty does not cover
accidental damage, water damage, or modification of the product by the user.
To claim a warranty repair, submit the purchase receipt to the Acme support
portal and receive a return merchandise authorization number within 3
business days.

## 4. Data Privacy

Acme only collects personal data that is required to process orders, deliver
products, and comply with legal obligations. Customer data is retained for a
maximum of 36 months after the last purchase and is made available to law
enforcement only when legally required. Acme never sells customer data to
third parties.

## 5. Employee Leave Policy

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid annual leave and 10 days of sick
leave per calendar year. Partial-year hires accrue leave proportionally from
their start date. Leave balances reset on January 1 each year. Up to 5 unused
leave days may be carried over to the following year, subject to manager
approval.

## 6. Return Window

An item may be returned within 30 days of purchase for a full refund only if
it is unused and in its original packaging. Items that show clear signs of
use, such as scratches, scuffs, or missing parts, may be subject to a
restocking fee of up to 15 percent.
